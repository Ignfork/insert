      FAQ

      What is the Insert Score?

      It's a 0 to 100 rating built from eight factors in a coin's live pair data: buy pressure, momentum, activity, liquidity depth (or curve depth on pump.fun), volume turnover, socials, age, and whether you'd be buying a dip or a vertical candle. 70 and up reads INSERT, 45 to 69 reads WAIT, and under 45 reads GAME OVER.

      Where does the data come from?

      The DexScreener public API, called straight from your browser each time you scan. Insert has no backend and no database, and it never shows made-up numbers. If a coin hasn't been indexed yet, you'll see that instead of a fake reading.

      Do I need to connect my wallet?

      No. Insert never asks for a wallet, a signature or a seed phrase. If anything claiming to be Insert asks you for one, it's a scam.

      How accurate is the curve calculator?

      It uses pump.fun's constant-product curve: 30 virtual SOL and 1,073,000,191 virtual tokens at launch, 793.1M tokens sold on the curve, and graduation at about 85 SOL. Other buys landing in the same block will move your real fill, which is what the slippage setting is for.

      Why didn't my coin load?

      Brand-new coins can take a short while to show up on DexScreener. Also check that you pasted a Solana mint address (32 to 44 characters). pump.fun addresses usually end in "pump".

      Is anything saved?

      Your watchlist, recent scans, checklist and session stats live in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere except the lookup request to DexScreener.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. Insert is a set of calculators and a heuristic. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Only insert what you can afford to lose.
